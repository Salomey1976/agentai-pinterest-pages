Privacy policy

Policy date: November 8, 2026

This policy covers the private AgentAI integration used by its owner to create Pins in the Pinterest account L0mey, and this public information website. The operator can be contacted at lomey.sa@gmail.com.

Information handled by the integration

The integration handles Pinterest access credentials (such as access and refresh tokens, where applicable), account and destination board identifiers, and content supplied by the owner: images, Pin titles, descriptions and destination links. Pinterest API responses, Pin identifiers and limited operational logs may also be processed to confirm publication and diagnose errors.

The integration is for the owner's account only. It does not provide registration or account connections for the public and does not request the owner's Pinterest password.

Purpose and storage

Information is processed to authorize requests, create Pins, confirm results and maintain the integration. Credentials and operational information are stored on the owner's Windows computer. This public website and its GitHub repository are not storage locations for credentials or private integration data.

Sharing and service providers

Content and the information required to create a Pin are sent to Pinterest. Published Pins are visible according to the account and board settings on Pinterest. The Pinterest folder is excluded from the configured general AgentAI Google Drive backup job. Information is not sold or shared for advertising.

Retention and deletion

Credentials are retained while the integration is in use. The operator can remove the locally stored credential using the disconnect utility and separately revoke access through Pinterest. Working copies of submitted content and publication records are retained only while needed for the owner's publishing workflow. A daily Windows task removes .log files older than 30 days in the designated logs folder and local .zip archives older than 90 days in the designated backups folder, without traversing subfolders. These are retention targets: when the computer is off or the operator is signed out, cleanup is deferred until the next available task run.

Deletion from the computer does not delete Pins already published to Pinterest. Published Pins must be managed separately on Pinterest.

Access and security

The private folder has restricted Windows permissions. The credential utility encrypts tokens using Windows DPAPI for the current Windows account. Tokens must not be embedded in these public pages, committed to the repository or written to diagnostic logs. Requests to Pinterest use HTTPS. No storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security.

Website visitors

These static pages do not use forms, analytics scripts, advertising trackers or application cookies. GitHub Pages hosts the website and may process technical request information, including IP addresses, under GitHub's privacy statement. Emailing the operator shares your email address and message with the operator and email service providers.

Your choices and requests

The owner can stop the integration and revoke its access using the available Pinterest account controls. For questions or requests to access, correct or delete information held by the operator, contact lomey.sa@gmail.com. Requests will be assessed and answered within 30 days; identity verification may be needed. Data held by Pinterest is governed by Pinterest's privacy policy.

Changes

This policy will be updated if the integration's purpose, data handling or service providers change. The policy date above identifies the current version.
